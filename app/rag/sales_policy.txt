Sales Policy

High-value customers are customers whose
annual purchase value exceeds ₹100,000.

Customers who make more than three purchases
within a quarter receive priority support.

Customers with annual purchases below ₹25,000
are classified as low-value customers.

Customers between ₹25,000 and ₹100,000
are classified as standard customers.

High-value customers are eligible for
dedicated account management.

Discounts above 15% require manager approval.
